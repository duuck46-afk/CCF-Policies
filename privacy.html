Crooked Compass Faction (CCF) Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Our Discord bot may process Discord usernames, user IDs, server IDs, roles, verification status, and information related to commands and interactions.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide server verification, assign roles, welcome members, support moderation, and maintain bot functionality.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell members' personal information. Information may be processed through Discord and services required to operate the bot.

4. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information. We retain information only as needed to operate the bot and provide its features.

5. Data Requests

Members may contact the CCF server administrators to ask questions or request deletion of information stored by the bot, where applicable.

6. Third-Party Services

Our bot operates through Discord. Discord's own privacy policy also applies to your use of Discord.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Crooked Compass Faction server administrators through our official Discord server.
