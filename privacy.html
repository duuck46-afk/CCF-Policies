Crooked Compass Faction

CCF Discord Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

Our Commitment: Crooked Compass Faction respects the privacy of its Discord community. This policy explains what information our Discord bot may process, why that information may be used, and what choices members have.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Crooked Compass Faction (CCF) Discord bot and its related services operating within Discord servers. Throughout this document, the terms "CCF," "we," "our," and "us" refer to the administrators and operators responsible for the bot.

This policy explains how information may be collected, accessed, processed, stored, retained, and deleted when members interact with the CCF bot.

By interacting with the bot, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this policy. If you do not agree with these practices, you may choose not to interact with the bot or request assistance from the server administrators.

2. Information We May Collect or Process

Depending on the features enabled and the permissions granted to the bot, certain Discord-related information may be processed. The information involved may include the following:

2.1 Discord Account Information

  • Discord usernames and display names.
  • Discord user IDs.
  • Server membership information.
  • Account-related identifiers made available through Discord.

2.2 Server and Role Information

  • Discord server IDs.
  • Server names and configuration information.
  • Member roles and role assignments.
  • Verification status, if verification features are enabled.
  • Information necessary to manage permitted server functions.

2.3 Bot Commands and Interactions

  • Commands submitted to the bot.
  • Button, menu, and other interaction responses.
  • Information needed to complete requested bot functions.
  • Technical error information used to troubleshoot problems.

2.4 Moderation Information

If moderation features are enabled, the bot may process information related to moderation actions, such as warnings, moderation commands, relevant user IDs, and action timestamps.

The bot should only access or retain moderation information necessary for its configured moderation functions.

2.5 Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

The CCF bot is not intended to request Discord passwords, payment card numbers, banking credentials, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information.

Members should never provide passwords, authentication codes, or private account credentials through bot commands or messages.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the CCF bot may be used only for legitimate community and operational purposes connected to its available features. These purposes may include:

  • Welcoming new members to the Discord server.
  • Supporting member verification procedures.
  • Assigning or removing Discord roles.
  • Managing permitted server activities.
  • Supporting moderation and enforcement of server rules.
  • Responding to commands and member interactions.
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving reliability.
  • Preventing misuse of bot features.
  • Maintaining the security and functionality of the bot.

We do not intend to use member information for unrelated purposes or to sell personal information.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be processed temporarily or stored depending on the bot's technical design and enabled features.

Where information is stored, reasonable efforts should be made to limit retention to the period necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Information may remain available for a limited period when necessary for server administration, moderation records, security, troubleshooting, or legal obligations.

The actual storage location, retention period, and deletion process depend on the hosting provider, database, and software configuration used to operate the bot.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

CCF does not sell or rent member information to advertisers, marketers, or unrelated third parties.

Information may be accessible to authorized CCF administrators when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or troubleshoot the bot.

Information may also be processed by service providers required to operate the bot, such as hosting providers or database services, if those services are used.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the security of the bot, or address serious misuse.

6. Discord and Third-Party Services

The CCF bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Discord independently processes information under its own policies. CCF does not control Discord's internal data practices.

If external hosting, database, analytics, or other services are used to operate the bot, those services may process information necessary to provide their functions.

Members should review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services when applicable.

7. Data Security

We recognize the importance of protecting information processed through the CCF bot. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Such safeguards may include limiting administrator access, protecting credentials, securing hosting environments, and keeping bot software and dependencies reasonably maintained.

However, no online service, software system, or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Member Privacy Rights and Requests

Members may contact the CCF server administrators with questions or requests concerning information associated with their Discord accounts.

Depending on the applicable law and information involved, members may request:

  • Information about data processed by the bot.
  • Correction of inaccurate information, where applicable.
  • Deletion of information stored by the bot, where applicable.
  • Clarification about how information is used.

Requests may require verification of the Discord account involved to prevent unauthorized access or deletion.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal reasons. We will explain when a request cannot reasonably be fulfilled.

9. Children's Privacy

The CCF bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

Members must meet Discord's applicable minimum age requirements and any additional age requirements imposed by applicable law.

If we become aware that information has been collected in a manner that violates applicable children's privacy requirements, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

10. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

If we become aware of a security incident affecting information processed by the CCF bot, we will take reasonable steps to investigate, limit further exposure, and address the issue.

Where required by applicable law, affected individuals or relevant authorities will be notified in accordance with applicable requirements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when bot features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will display a revised "Last Updated" date. Members are encouraged to review this page periodically.

12. Contact and Privacy Questions

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about the CCF bot, contact the Crooked Compass Faction server administrators through the official CCF Discord server.

Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive account credentials when making a privacy request.

13. Policy Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the CCF Discord bot and the information processed through its configured features. It does not replace Discord's own privacy policy or the policies of independent third-party services.
